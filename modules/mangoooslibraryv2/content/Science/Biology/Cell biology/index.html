Cell Biology

Cell Biology is the branch of biology that studies the fundamentals of cell theory. The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of all forms of life or organisms

What is a cell?

A cell is the fundamental structural-basis for any complexer life-forms on earth.

Cell theory consists of 3 primary principles:

- All living organisms are composed of one or more cells.
- The cell is the basic unit of life.
- Every cell arises from a pre-existing cell.

The third point is particularly significant because it implies that cells are never created spontaneously. Every cell you possess today is descended from cells that existed continuously throughout your development, which in turn descended from cells in your parents, and so on through billions of years of evolution.

But what exactly is life?

The closests definition of life we have is built on these main principles:

- Metabolism
- Homeostasis
- Growth
- Response to stimuli
- Reproduction
- Evolution

For example, a single bacterial cell can abord nutrients, convert that into energy, maintain internal chemical conditions, repair molecular damage, reproduce and evolve through mutation.

The purpose of the cell is to contain and regulate thousands of chemical reactions happening within - simulationsly.

If these molecules were to disperse freely throughout the environment, diffusion would rapidly dilute them making these complex living conditions for cells to form impossible.

The cell membrane

The cell membrane (also known as plasma membrane) is a thin layer of tissue that surrounds the cell, acting as a barrier to manage the passthrough of molecules, ions, or other small particles, and protect the interior cell.

The structural component of a cell membrane is the phospholipid bilayer. A phospholipid is made out of amphipathic molecules. This molecule has a hydrophilic (water-loving) head, and hydrophobic (water-fearing) tail.
